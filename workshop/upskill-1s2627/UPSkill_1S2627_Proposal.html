III. Objectives

  1. To encourage applicants — especially absolute beginners — to know more about data science using companies they already recognize (e.g. SM, Jollibee, BDO, Globe, Meralco).
  2. To give applicants a glimpse of Guild workshops through a simple pipeline story: clean a table → ask a question → filter an answer, without requiring prior coding experience.
  3. To give hands-on experience with a real-world (offline) PSE snapshot in one cohesive ~3-hour session: spreadsheet foundations, then a short guided Colab mini-screen on the same familiar companies.
